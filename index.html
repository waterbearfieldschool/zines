✂️ Zine Studio

Make an 8-page mini-zine from a single sheet of 8.5×11 paper — printed on one side only, folded, with one small cut. Each page is 2.75″ × 4.25″.

Editing

  • + text / + image buttons above each page add elements; you can also drag & drop image files onto a page, or paste a copied image with Ctrl+V.
  • Click an element to select it, drag to move, use the round handles to resize, and the red handle above to rotate (hold Shift for free rotation).
  • Double-click text to edit it. Fonts, size, color and alignment appear in the toolbar when selected.
  • Work is autosaved in your browser. Use Save file to keep a portable copy.
  • 📖 Flip through shows an animated preview of the finished booklet — click pages to turn them.
  • Every page starts with a small page number at the bottom — it's an ordinary text element, so click it to restyle, move, or delete it.

Printing

Print at 100% scale (not "fit to page"), single-sided, with margins set to None or Minimum. Most printers can't print to the very edge, so keep important content a little away from page edges.

Want a PDF? Use the same 🖨 button and choose Save as PDF as the destination in the print dialog — you'll get a perfect vector PDF of the sheet.

Folding (after printing)

  1. Fold in half — long edge to long edge ("hot-dog"). Unfold.
  2. Fold in half short-edge to short-edge, then fold each half back to the middle, making 8 panels. Unfold everything.
  3. Fold in half short-edge to short-edge and cut along the center crease from the folded edge to the middle of the sheet — the slit should span only the middle two panels. (The fold guides mark it with ✂.)
  4. Unfold, then refold hot-dog with the printing on the outside.
  5. Push the two ends toward the middle — the slit opens into a diamond and the panels form a plus-sign. Wrap them flat into a booklet with the cover on front. Crease well!
